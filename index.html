Sleep on it

Small, slow games for the last minutes of the day. Each one gives your mind something gentle to follow until it lets go.

The games

  • Ants

    Choose colour boxes and send ants to carry a pixel picture home, one cube at a time. A growing collection of pixel pictures, from a snail to a castle.

    Play Ants

  • Watching paint dry

    A fresh coat on the wall slowly turns from glossy to matte, drip by drip.

    In the works.

How it plays

Everything runs on your phone. Add the page to your home screen and it opens like an app, plays offline, and works in airplane mode.

Each game moves at an unhurried pace and keeps its colours easy to see in the dark. Your progress stays in your browser, on your device.
